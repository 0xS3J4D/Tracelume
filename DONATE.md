### Safety
- Send each coin **only on its own network**; funds sent on the wrong network may be lost.
- Always check the address against this file in the official repository,
  **github.com/0xS3J4D/tracelume**. Maintainers will never DM you a different address.
- The QR codes encode exactly the addresses shown above (`bitcoin:`, `ethereum:`, `monero:` URIs).
